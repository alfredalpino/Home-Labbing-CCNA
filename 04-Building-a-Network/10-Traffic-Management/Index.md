# Traffic Management

Control behavior under congestion — don’t pretend bandwidth is infinite.

## Analogy

> Traffic management is the **bridge police kit**: overall policy ([[QoS]]), velvet-rope metering ([[Traffic-Shaping]]), and ambulance lanes ([[Packet-Prioritization]]).
