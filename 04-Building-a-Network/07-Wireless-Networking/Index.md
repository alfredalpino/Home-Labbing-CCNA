# Wireless Networking

How engineers design, secure, and operate **[[WLAN]]** access — from 802.11 standards and [[Access Points]] architecture to RF surveys, roaming, and related radio technologies.

## Analogy

> Wired switching is **plumbing with copper pipes**. Wireless networking is **air-traffic control for radios**: standards define the aircraft ([[WiFi-Standards]]), towers and HQ run the airport ([[Access-Points-Controllers]]), placement and channels keep planes from colliding ([[AP-Placement-and-Coverage]], [[Channel-Planning]]), surveys are the weather radar ([[Wireless-Site-Surveys]]), and security is the TSA plus sealed cargo ([[Wireless-Security]], [[VPN]]).
