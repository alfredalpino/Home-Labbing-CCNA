# Network Devices

The hardware (and virtual appliances) that forward, concentrate, and translate traffic.

## Analogy

> Devices are **city infrastructure roles**:
> - [[Hub]] — a noisy megaphone intersection (everyone hears everything)
> - [[Switches]] — smart intersections with street signs (MAC learning)
> - [[Routers]] — highway on‑ramps between cities (IP routing)
> - [[Modems]] — translators at the city border to the ISP’s foreign language
> - [[Access Points]] — radio taxi stands bridging wireless riders onto wired streets

## Devices

| Note | Forwards by | Typical layer |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| [[Routers]] | IP networks | L3 |
| [[Switches]] | MAC addresses | L2 |
| [[Hub]] | Bitwise repeat (obsolete) | L1 |
| [[Modems]] | Signal modulation + often gateway | L1–L3 combo in SOHO |
| [[Access Points]] | Wireless ↔ wired bridge | L2 (+ RF) |
