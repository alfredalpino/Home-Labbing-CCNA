# Network Attacks

For this roadmap slice we focus on availability attacks engineers must **recognize and mitigate** — especially [[DoS DDoS]].

> Defensive mastery only: understand symptoms, triage, and mitigations. Do not practice attacking systems you don’t own.

## Analogy

> Attacks on networks are like **assaults on a city’s transport**:
> - DoS/DDoS = flooding every highway on-ramp until ambulances can’t move
> - Other attack classes (spoofing, MITM, credential stuffing) appear elsewhere in security study — here we go deep on floods
