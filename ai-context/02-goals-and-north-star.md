# Goals & North Star

## Career north star

Become a hireable **Network Engineer / NOC / Network Analyst** who can own L1–L2 triage, L3 fundamentals (static/OSPF), ACLs, ISP/CPE edge playbooks, and document under SLA — with proof in a **public lab monorepo**, not just certificates.

**Geographic target:** GCC — **UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait** — **onsite or remote**.

**Not the north star (Month 1 / near term):** software architect, full-stack product builder, DSA grind, K8s platform engineer, AWS-deep specialist, AI red team / prompt-eng career paths.

## Role targets (apply language)

Use these titles when searching and applying:

- Network Engineer
- NOC Engineer / NOC Analyst
- Network Analyst
- Network Support Engineer
- Network Operations (L1/L2)

Accept adjacent titles only if the JD is **network operations first** (routing/switching, monitoring, tickets, ISP/enterprise edge) — not pure software or pure security career pivots.

## Success metrics (how to judge advice)

An AI suggestion is **good** if it:

1. Moves a lab to “public + documented,” or
2. Improves application materials with **true** facts, or
3. Increases qualified applications without breaking the Month-1 DROP list.

An AI suggestion is **bad** if it:

1. Adds a new certification chase before labs ship, or
2. Opens a DROP-list track (DSA deep, full K8s, Design System, etc.), or
3. Invents experience, metrics, or employers.
